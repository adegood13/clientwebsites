About

Hi, I'm Amanda.

I picked up a camera in 2001 — before anyone had a cell phone in their pocket, before "digital" was a given. I shot film. I learned in the dark. I'm still chasing the same kind of frame I was chasing then: a quiet one, where the light does the work and you don't have to perform.

For twenty-something years now, this has been the job: maternity in the soft hour after lunch, newborns sleeping through their entire session, one-year-olds wearing more cake than they ate. I'm a mom myself. My studio is built for parents who haven't slept and babies who'd rather be home. Heat at 80°. Tea on the counter. A changing table you don't have to ask about. Two refrigerators if you're feeding.

Nothing about a session here is rushed. Nothing about it is staged in the way that ages out of style. We make pictures you'll look at when they're 22.

— Amanda

Common questions

Things parents ask before they book.

When should I book my newborn session?

As soon as you have a due date, even tentatively. I shoot newborns 5–14 days postpartum, and dates fill 2–3 months ahead. Once baby arrives, we firm up the actual date.

How long is a newborn session?

About three hours. Most of that is feeding, snuggling, and waiting for sleep. The actual photography happens in short windows — that's why we don't rush.

Do I need to bring anything?

For newborns, just baby and feeding supplies. The studio has wraps, headbands, props, a changing table, a sound machine, and a microwave. Bring a snack if you'd like. For maternity, I'll send a styling guide ahead of time.

Can I bring siblings & partner?

Always — at no extra charge. We typically do family shots in the first 30 minutes so older siblings can leave with one parent if they're done.

Where do you shoot?

In my studio at 1913 Huguenot Rd, Suite 100, North Chesterfield. The space is built for this — temperature controlled, bright, private.

How does payment work?

A retainer holds your date (varies by session — $200 maternity, $300 newborn, $400 first-year plan). The balance is due the day of your session. Card, ACH, or check.

What about prints & albums?

Your gallery includes a full print release. I also offer hand-bound albums and gallery wall art if you'd like me to handle that for you — pricing in your gallery.
