Allen & Michael · co-owners
The story

A master colorist and a former occupational therapist walk into a salon.

Allen Coleman has been doing hair for longer than most of our regulars have lived in Tampa. Master colorist, master cutter, the guy other stylists send their hardest correctives to. He runs the floor.

Michael Berthelette spent his career as an occupational therapist before he and Allen built Platinum together. Different background, same instinct: pay attention to the human in the chair. He runs the business — the booking system that texts you when your color's about to fade, the staff who actually pick up the phone, the espresso machine that's never empty.

Twelve chairs, fifteen people, one standard. That's Platinum.

— Allen & Michael
