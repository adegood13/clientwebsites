FAQ

Things people ask before they book.

Do you need water and electricity?

Nope. Our truck carries both. All we need from you is roughly 8 feet on either side of the car.

What if it rains?

Light rain we work through. If it's coming down hard we'll text you to reschedule — no fee, no hassle.

How long does it take?

Quick Shine is ~90 minutes. Full Golden is around 3 hours. Deep Restore is 5+. We'll never rush a job.

Can you come to my office?

Yes — that's actually most of our weekday work. Just give us a parking spot.

Do you do trucks & large SUVs?

Every day. Add roughly $20–$50 to package pricing depending on size — we'll quote you exactly before booking.

Pet hair / heavy soiling?

We handle it, but it's an add-on. We'll see your photos in chat and quote a fair fee before we book the visit.

How do I pay?

Card (Visa/MC/Amex), Apple Pay, or cash. A $25 deposit holds your slot and applies to the total.

Tips?

Never expected, always appreciated. Most customers tip 10–20%.
