Common questions

Things people ask.

Do I need to be home?

Not at all. Most clients leave a key with us or use a smart lock code. We'll text when we arrive and when we leave.

Do you bring supplies?

Yes — vacuums, mops, microfiber, products. If you have a specific product you want us to use, leave it on the counter and we'll use yours.

Pets okay?

Always. Pet-safe products by default, and we'll work around your routine. Tell us their names — we'll be friends by visit two.

Same cleaner every time?

That's the goal. Sometimes life happens, but we'll always tell you in advance if your regular cleaner can't make it.

What if I'm not happy?

Text us within 24 hours and we come back to fix it. No invoice, no argument. That's the guarantee.

How much does it actually cost?

Get the instant quote and you'll see real numbers. Roughly: 1-bed apartment from $129. 3-bed home from $189. 4-bed home from $239. Deep cleans run about double.

Are you insured?

Yes. $2M general liability, every cleaner background-checked. Certificate available on request before your first visit.

Where do you serve?

Cincinnati and the immediate suburbs — Westwood, Price Hill, Northside, Walnut Hills, Mt. Lookout, Hyde Park, Oakley, Norwood, Clifton, Mariemont, Madeira, and out to Anderson and Florence on request.
