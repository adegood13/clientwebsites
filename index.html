The story

Daphne's was named after a dog.

The original Daphne — a sweet, patient mixed breed — belonged to my mother. When the shop opened in midtown decades ago, naming it after her was the obvious move. Some of our oldest clients still ask about her by name.

I'm Veronica Saulsberry, and I've run this shop and the family that came after it for years. We've groomed multiple generations of Memphis dogs — the puppy you brought in last week is the great-great-grand-something of the dog we groomed for your mom in the '90s.

That's not a marketing line. That's the actual job. We learn your dog. We learn what makes them nervous. We figure out the haircut that works on their actual coat — not the breed-standard photo. And we send them home tired, clean, and themselves.

— Veronica

Common questions

Things people ask.

How long does a groom take?

Spa Bath: about an hour. Full Groom: 2 hours. Quick Tidy: 30 min. We never crate-dry, so the bigger the dog and thicker the coat, the longer it takes — that's the trade for a better finish.

What about anxious dogs?

Tell us in the booking notes. We'll do a meet-and-greet first if needed. We never muzzle unless safety requires it — and even then, we call you to talk through it first.

What vaccinations do you require?

For grooming: DHPP and rabies. For boarding: add bordetella (kennel cough). Send your records ahead by email or text.

Cats?

Yes — we have a cat groomer who comes in by appointment. Call to schedule. Pricing depends on coat length and condition.

What's your matting policy?

We do humane mat removal first, but if a coat is severely matted we'll talk to you about a fresh-start shave-down. We never demat a painfully matted dog "for cosmetic reasons."

Can I stay during the groom?

You're welcome to wait in the lobby — many clients do. Most dogs do better when their person is out of sight, though.

Boarding photos?

Always. We'll text you 1–2 photos a day. Two-night stays and longer get a play-yard video.

How do I pay?

Card (Visa/MC/Amex), Apple Pay, or cash at pickup. Boarding requires a $25 deposit at booking.
