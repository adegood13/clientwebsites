The story

The shop that didn't move.

We opened the doors at 6162 Clinton Highway in March of 2003. We're still there. Same address, same standard, same people who've been tattooing Knoxville since flip phones. The street has changed. We haven't.

Real questions

Things people ask before they sit.

How much does a tattoo cost?

Shop minimum is $80. Most small custom pieces land $150–$350. Bigger work gets quoted by session — usually $150 an hour. We'll quote you straight after we see size and complexity.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome for ear piercings, small flash, and last-minute slots. Anything custom needs a free consult so we have time to draw it right.

How does the deposit work?

$50 deposit holds your consult slot and goes toward your tattoo. Refundable up to 48 hours before. One free reschedule.

Can I bring a friend?

One person, please. Our chairs are tight and the artists need to focus. No kids in the tattoo area.

How old do I have to be?

18 to get tattooed. 16 with notarized parental consent for piercing (no genital, navel, or nipple under 18 regardless). Bring valid photo ID — no exceptions.

Does it hurt?

Yes, some places more than others. Eat before you come. Hydrate. Don't drink the night before. Don't take blood thinners (including ibuprofen) the day of.

Aftercare?

You'll go home with printed instructions and a number to text. Two weeks of out-of-the-sun, no soaking. We'd rather you ask a "stupid" question than guess.

Cover-ups?

Yes, frequently. Bring a clear photo of the existing piece for your consult — bigger and darker is what we work with.

Touch-ups?

One free touch-up within 30 days if you've followed aftercare. After that, $40–$80 depending on size.
