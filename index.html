Things people ask.

Do I need an appointment?

No — we're walk-in friendly seven days a week, especially evenings. For larger or custom work, book a free consult so your artist has time to draw and you have time to look at it.

How much does a tattoo cost?

Shop minimum is $80. Most small custom pieces land $150–$350. Bigger work is quoted per session ($150/hr typical). We'll give you an honest number after we see the size and complexity.

How does the deposit work?

$50 deposit holds your booked slot and goes toward your tattoo. Refundable up to 48 hours before. Reschedule once for free.

What about the waiver?

We'll send a digital waiver after you book — sign on your phone before you arrive. New Mexico law requires ID showing you're 18+. No exceptions, even with parental consent.

Do you do touch-ups?

One free touch-up within 30 days if you've followed aftercare. After that, $40–$80 depending on size.

Cover-ups?

Yes, frequently. Bring a clear photo of the existing piece for your consult — we'll tell you what's possible.

What's piercing?

SE shop only. Walk-in for ear piercings; book ahead for everything else. Implant-grade titanium for fresh piercings.

Healing & aftercare?

You go home with printed instructions and a number to text. Two weeks of protect-from-sun, no soaking. We'd rather you ask a "stupid" question than guess.

Ready when you are.

Book a free consult. We'll quote it honestly, draw it carefully, and tattoo it well.
