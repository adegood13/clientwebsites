FAQ

Things people ask.

Do you need water and electricity?

Nope. Self-contained truck — water tanks, generator, hot water, the works. We need 8 feet on either side of the car, that's it.

What about 110° summers?

We work shaded sides, start early when we can, and run cold-water packs in the truck. We'd rather pause than rush a job in the heat.

Monsoon mess?

That's why "Monsoon Mud Rescue" is on the add-on menu. Undercarriage included. Get on the calendar fast — bookings spike after every storm.

How long does it take?

Quick Wash: ~75 min. Full Detail: ~3.5 hrs. Desert Restore: 5+ hrs. We won't rush a job to make a window.

Can you come to my office?

Most of our weekday work is exactly that. Tell us where to park.

Trucks & large SUVs?

Daily — F-150s, Tahoes, Suburbans, Sequoias. Add $25–$60 to package pricing depending on size. We'll quote exactly before booking.

How do I pay?

Card (Visa/MC/Amex), Apple Pay, or cash. A $25 deposit holds your slot and applies to the total.

Tipping?

Never expected. Always appreciated. Most customers tip 10–20%.

Saturday plans don't include the wash bay.
